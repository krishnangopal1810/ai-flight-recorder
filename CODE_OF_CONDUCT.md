# Code of Conduct

AI Flight Recorder is built around difficult incidents. We want people to be able to share failures, uncertainty, and mistakes without being diminished for them.

## Our standard

Be curious, specific, and kind. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Welcome questions asked in good faith. Make room for different backgrounds, communication styles, time zones, and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or retaliating against someone who raises a concern.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, Discussions, events, and private project communication. It also applies when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `krishnangopal1810` through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue. Include links or screenshots only when doing so is safe for everyone involved.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, issue a warning, temporarily suspend access, or permanently remove someone from project spaces. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter.

Good-faith reports and appeals will not be punished. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.
